# H15 representation comparison proposal

Prospective and not yet executed. The native pair pilot passed its independently
frozen source-support gate. This proposal requires a genuine receive-prefix
proof and the independently frozen fixed-reference mapping coverage study to
pass before full material or training. Failure at either gate rejects the
corresponding recipe; no reference/date/universe substitution is allowed.

The information hypothesis is that an execution-confirmed external price
impulse and its owned target response history distinguish continuing repricing,
observed absorption and source refutation on the300s five-tick problem. The
episode persists after initial following. Receive chronology establishes
availability, not economic causation or exchange-clock leadership. The latest
mark may discard slower buildup across multiple episodes; this is a falsifiable
limitation, not a reason to enlarge windows after results.

All material H15 predictors come from the native experimental
`PeerTradeInformation` FeatureModule and DatasetWriter. Python selects existing
native columns, joins exact observation identities and evaluates CatBoost. It
calculates no new features. Seven numeric fields are emitted once and shared
between K and J; the only difference is two categorical coding fields.

The fixed target universe is the original16 stocks. Every target references
2330 except2330 itself references2317. This designated reference is not a
selected best peer, assumed beta-one forecast or verified sector taxonomy.
Eligibility retains original ordinary-share/observability rules. Missing raw
inputs or BasicInfo are skipped without replacement under a frozen common
cohort; event sparsity never permits a target to be removed after evaluation.

The four primary arms are a fresh legacy full control B, a bounded native local
conditioning representation C, C+K and C+J. B retains its immutable old inputs
as a scientific reference; its known raw-scale issues do not qualify it for
cross-symbol-normalized admission. C is selected prospectively by information
roles rather than oldcore27 or feature count. Its exact descriptors, original
source vintage, windows, scales and sentinel rules must be source-audited and
fixed before any comparison or label-based screening. It represents price
geometry, session/price anchors, local known demand and observed supply response,
and causal activity/response surprise. Reuse existing native outputs and one
existing seat per role; no window search, parameter expansion or near-duplicate
variants. Preserve original feature bits and availability when selecting from
the immutable historical projection; current warmup fixes cannot recode it.

C+K versus C tests the contribution of external plus event-anchored target
history jointly. It cannot isolate external information from the adaptive
target baseline. C+J versus C+K tests explicit chronology/alignment categorical
coding; K already contains the joined numeric history and therefore supplies
no economic-causality or complete-join-removal experiment. C versus B diagnoses
whether a replacement loses conditioning context. A candidate that improves C
but remains below B has not established a better complete representation.

The inherited original frozen judge applies unchanged to all four arms:
original30s origins mature at60/120/180/300s, native pure-mid inclusive+/-5tick
endpoint labels, chronological TRAIN/ES/calibration/development roles, held-label
symbols, equal-symbol/day recency weighting, fixed MultiClass CatBoost GPU0
procedure, primary300s metrics and all acceptance/paired-day/group/tail guards.
The unsupported conditional-direction methodology study is not adopted.
No model-family search, HPO or alternate loss is part of this FE comparison.

At most four primary fits, serialized by root with the global GPU lock. Reuse
the original baseline matrices and one shared H15 native role cache; C and both
augmented arms use the same C descriptors and bits. Do not physically duplicate
datasets or K/J numeric columns. All arms must pass exact key/role/all-horizon
label signature equality and native input provenance before the evaluation
contract freezes. Freeze the full profile, procedure, sources, artifacts and
environment before the first fresh B fit. Score every arm on calibration and
persist nomination before any development comparison. Predeclare both C+K/C
and J/K contrasts under the same judge; diagnostic improvements cannot bypass
the nomination or complete-representation acceptance rules.

Report directional information with conditional direction and directional tail
metrics, magnitude with large-move AP/absolute-move ranking, and joint event
ranking separately. A direction-only improvement may motivate later research,
but the admitted complete representation still needs magnitude and direction
power. Session/horizon base-rate variation, signal decay, seen/held symbols and
paired-day uncertainty must remain visible. No best horizon or symbol is chosen
from exposed development outcomes. No extra primary complement or secondary
fit grid is authorized by this proposal.

Development dates are already exposed research material. Final success still
requires the separately frozen future sealed OOS role with sufficient actual
dates after2026-10-01; no missing days, synthetic events or retrospective seal
can supply that evidence. Engineering tests, source support and causal replay
proof are prerequisites, not predictive admission.
