The fixed rules use native touch imbalance and five-second trade-flow imbalance, continuation direction, June absolute-score q95 thresholds, and July through August 26 research validation. ## Research-system consequence

This study separates capture integrity, planned coverage, directional information and friction sufficiently to close its registered question. Together with R0/A/Q and H1–H3, it supports carrying these distinctions into research feedback instead of responding to every failure with a candidate mutation. It does not establish a universal early screen, safe rejection of conditionally useful features, an optimal stage order, or improved automatic allocation. The negative and partly dependent corpus cannot establish those claims.

Reopen only for a demonstrated material source/semantic/cost defect, or independent evidence for a distinct information, conditional representation or causal execution mechanism with plausible economic scale. Register that new question and its decision criteria before measurement. A nearby setting, a pooled positive midpoint mean or an unsupported hope that learning will help is insufficient. No reopening measurement is identified by this result itself.
