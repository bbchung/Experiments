## Question and comparison

Should the unconditional opportunity-to-cost proxy exclude stocks before feature production, or remain only in ranking and diagnostics? The control retains the historical hard predicate. The alternative omits only that predicate while preserving its ranking contribution and the other selection rules. Three frozen chronological cases reuse their exact control roots and fit one alternative each. Qualification, fitting and numeric calibration may change as consequences of the changed population; this is a whole-procedure comparison, not individual-stock attribution.

Each arm uses its own calibration of the same first-declared q0.8/TTL30 policy, eighteen fixed H days, realistic registered costs and one shared 10-million-TWD account. All six cells receive diagnostic execution regardless of initial predictive admission. Ordinary frozen bank construction and initial diagnostics still evaluate sixteen rules; there is no H policy search or policy substitution, control refit, date substitution or extra seed. The generic recipe differs from `baseline.yaml`; W5 cannot directly authorize changing that canonical control.

The primary endpoint is the equal-case mean paired daily net cash divided by initial credit. Independent replication consideration requires complete valid evidence, a positive one-sided 95% paired block-bootstrap lower bound, at least 1 bp/day improvement, positive differences in at least two cases, and one alternative satisfying the separately frozen continuation criteria. Passing would authorize considering replication only. These are exposed, dependent research-validation cases; the bootstrap does not establish population-wide or final-OOS reliability.

Before selecting another experiment, we asked whether one selector should jointly govern the learning population and executable roster. A single exploratory accounting, fixed after aggregate outcomes were known but before group computation, partitions **every case, day and prebound symbol** into retained, added and displaced sets. All 1,206 observed symbol-day rows remain included. Per case:

The decomposition cannot identify retraining, calibration or account-competition effects, and absent-arm symbols have unknown counterfactual payoffs. No finer strata, favorable-stock rescue or new fit follows.

## Research-system decision

1. **System lesson:** broader populations, larger models and greater activity are distinct from useful economic information. The diagnostic obtained complete six-cell economic accounting for the fixed comparison, including real inactivity; closure depends on the predeclared investment criterion.
2. **Changed assumptions:** the narrow universe as a sufficient explanation of failure is weakened. Hard-gate optimality, adequate learning and absence of raw information remain unproved.
3. **Stage order:** causal material, source identity, semantic parity and complete accounting retain their validity responsibilities. Both arms had identical diagnostic access, so W5 does not establish diagnostic ordering or predictive-window necessity.
4. **Feedback destination:** economic evidence reaches the upstream universe decision. The responsible layer remains ambiguous because population, qualification, learning, calibration and account interactions change together. Preserve those competing explanations.
5. **Proxy control:** neither 191 trips, more retained trees nor completed execution earns promotion. A finite investment failure can close without a universal causal diagnosis.
6. **Current change:** make the closure consequential. Retain the canonical control and validated correctness repairs; promote no selector, learner or automatic routing change from this result.
7. **Minimum further experiment:** none is currently justified. Formal checks, independent primary verification and exposure reconciliation are complete. The strongest proposed final attribution iteration is complete and did not earn a new design review.

The remaining concrete workflow question is whether predictive admission withholds a fixed policy with supported, materially positive later native economics. W5 supplies no such witness, and W4 never activated its window comparison. No independently motivated new formulation and relevant uninspected adjudicator have been identified. This question stays open without manufacturing another candidate batch. Reopening the closed direction requires a demonstrated validity defect or materially new, independently motivated evidence with a comparison that could change the investment decision.
