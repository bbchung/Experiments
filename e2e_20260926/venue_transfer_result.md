H1 did not advance. Both frozen score arms failed the registered 90% common-support gate, so the primary conclusion is **inconclusive support**, with strongly negative observed conditional economics. This is research-validation evidence from already exposed dates, not final OOS, realized PnL, or evidence that the entire missing population is unprofitable. No model, feature, policy, universe, or workflow-performance change was promoted.

The [registered design](venue_transfer.yaml) evaluated existing `price` and `price_mkt` stock scores with their stored prior-calibration q95 thresholds, side-separated outcome-independent 1800-second spacing, 50 ms receive delay, strictly prior quotes, a 3-second age limit, and visible capacity for two stock lots versus one nominal 2000-share SSF contract. Primary exits used the archived stock-native stopping time; fixed 1800-second exits were a declared sensitivity. Both independently spaced sides were reported separately and jointly; the joint average is not a trading portfolio.

The first attempt exposed an implementation error: duplicate native timestamps anywhere in a stock-day incorrectly invalidated every selected intent in that partition. It was stopped before economic inspection. Its registration, source, exposure claims and 156 partial parts remain in [the original attempt](../../runs/research/venue-transfer-20260926/), explicitly marked as an implementation failure with no economic conclusion. The preregistered v2 correction accepts only unique selected timestamps; selected duplicates remain unknown even if their outcomes happen to match. Fourteen focused tests and targeted Ruff checks pass. A subsequent report-only correctness guard rejects changed registration bytes or protocol values before standalone reporting; executed source and completed result hashes were preserved in `report-tooling-hardening.yaml`.

Archived label logs confirm native BIN inputs on all 60 study dates, whereas this diagnostic reads CSV. A predeclared recording falsification forced BIN-only and CSV-only inputs through the same frozen native binary and `MdWriter`, with the same `TseInfo`, no `TwseFilter`, isolated input roots and unique output directories. All records were byte-, key-, and value-identical for 2330, 2317 and 2454 on June 3 and August 3: **488,066 records per format across six cases**. Configurations, source hashes, logs and canonical outputs are in `recording-probe/`. This weakens a recording-format explanation on those cases; it does not certify all dates, symbols, historical parser vintages or feature-production lineage.

Remaining lineage limits matter independently of the negative economics. Frozen scores omit full model provenance and native origin keys. Historical origin exports may omit unresolved EOF samples, with unknown impact inside this earlier entry cutoff. Prototype price paths used finite origin quotes from one-flip label outputs; dependence on later validity cannot be excluded without reconstructing the producer. Complete historical adjusted-deliverable terms are unavailable, so ordinary roots assume nominal 2000 shares. Positive adoption would have required raw/native reconstruction, full causal population accounting and native execution; this family did not reach that stage.

After the full result, the already approved H2 structural pairs CDF/2330, DHF/2317 and DVF/2454 were described in `structural-comparators-cdf-dhf-dvf.csv`; they were not searched for H1 winners. Primary resolved samples per arm were only 12/12, 18/27 and 41/46, respectively. Their observed SSF crossing was roughly 4.9, 22–24 and 22 bps, with fees about 0.61, 2.44–2.46 and 0.53 bps. These describe structurally different friction environments for the pre-existing H2 design, not a promoted subset or a new H1 success. The H2 protocol was not changed.

The useful negative evidence is narrow: transporting these legacy frozen scores into nominal standard SSFs under the declared aggressive quote policy did not establish economically useful performance. Cheaper fees alone are insufficient; quoted spread, available size, freshness and directional information must be addressed causally. The next investigation should test a separately registered native information/execution mechanism, rather than tune these thresholds or choose a favorable observed symbol.
