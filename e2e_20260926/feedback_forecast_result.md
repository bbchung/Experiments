# W3: does execution feedback improve intervention forecasts?

The registered two-case comparison passes: adding canonical parent execution attribution reduces mean normalized forecast loss in both cases and beats the zero-change comparator. The benefit is fragile and does **not** justify changing automatic research allocation, stage order, qualification or promotion. Both arms badly understate the realized cash changes.

This tests a research-interface hypothesis using retained outcomes; it adds no market experiment. The eligible corpus comprises Q1/A (quality-only feature selection with the registered refit/calibration) and H3/R0 (timed-exit price/TIF change). These are the campaign's completed controlled full-native intervention families with audited paired cash/trip outcomes. They share dates and related models. Investigators already knew their results; fresh reader contexts received only masked parent evidence and the registered intervention.

## Results and falsification

| Case | Common feedback | Augmented feedback | Zero-change | Relative mean reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Q1 | 0.344721 | 0.297275 | 0.512829 | 13.76% |
| H3 | 0.131550 | 0.112589 | 0.151617 | 14.41% |
| Equal-case mean | 0.238135 | 0.204932 | 0.332223 | 13.94% |

These are forecast losses, not trading returns. The underlying paired native changes remain Q1: **−19,254.520 TWD / +27 trips**; H3: **+4,210.765 TWD / +14 trips**. Neither original intervention earned economic promotion.

Adversarial checks substantially limit the positive result:

- Q1's cash loss improves from 0.543351 to 0.422945, but trip-count loss worsens from 0.146091 to 0.171605. More information does not improve every relevant prediction.
- H3 cash-only loss is 0.183158 under augmented feedback, worse than cash-only zero-change at 0.175961. Its composite win over zero also depends on predicting increased trip count. The registered composite remains a pass; it does not establish superior economic expectations.
- Both cases favor augmented feedback in two of three draw pairs. Removing H3's strongest favorable pair reverses its average comparison. This is a post-score fragility diagnostic, not a new gate or permission to discard a draw.
- Q1's actual cash delta is below every emitted q10; H3's is above every q90. Q1's trip increase also exceeds every q90. All emitted cash intervals miss these two realized outcomes. Repeated forecasts are not independent market observations, so this is not an estimated general calibration rate.
- The two dependent, outcome-exposed families and three draws per cell cannot establish temporal robustness, calibrated research decisions or useful behavior in the ordinary proposer. No p-value, independent-market confidence interval or OOS claim is made.

Calls total 152.337 seconds. Common/augmented input totals are 64,315/69,442 tokens and output totals 2,115/3,024 tokens; Provider-reported reasoning counts are 1,751/2,675; they are not added to output totals in this report. ## Research decision

Retain the correctness-required execution evidence interface. The pass permits designing a separately registered prospective shadow comparison at the existing proposal boundary; the fragility and interval misses make automatic authority premature. Do not add a controller, tune prompts/models on these cases, rerun candidate variants, or generate a market experiment merely to supply that comparison. A future test needs an independently justified intervention, a pre-outcome expectation, a matched settled endpoint and unchanged actual execution decisions.

The seven-question architecture update is in [the canonical research-system record](../../../docs/research_system_20260927.md). Stage necessity, all-window predictive admission, earlier native diagnostics and optimal action allocation remain separate unvalidated hypotheses. No final candidate or untouched final OOS is created by this result.
