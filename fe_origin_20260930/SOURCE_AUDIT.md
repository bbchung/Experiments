## Event-count ratio and the model domain

`NetDepthRemovalExcessTradeRatio::on_book` calculates the positive aggregate displayed-depth loss that exceeds intervening printed volume, then divides its EMA by the intervening trade-volume EMA. Both EMAs advance once per book update. `Ema::insert` multiplies the old value by `1-alpha` on every zero-volume update. Consequently, the denominator can approach zero even while the numerator receives new removals; a strictly nonzero denominator does not ensure a numerically useful ratio. It also makes the effective physical-time memory depend on the book-message rate.

Native 2026-02-03 data for 2609 contain 21 finite observations above the float32 range, with a maximum near `6.69e145`. This finding is directly reproducible from the untouched native Parquet. The initial input check failed explicitly rather than silently converting these finite values into positive infinity. A train-only whole-column exclusion is now predeclared, with a separate model-domain report; no original values or row population are repaired.

A candidate replacement is a bounded share of displayed-depth loss unexplained by prints, coupled with its total activity/support, under a physical-time window. The motivation is to distinguish low printed activity from a supported supply-side response without allowing a vanishing denominator to dominate. Snapshot data do not identify true cancellations: net replenishment, level movement and aggregation remain alternatives. This candidate is not yet a validated predictive improvement and the running native binary is unchanged.

Sources: `src/oms/modules/feature/order_flow/intensity/net_depth_removal_excess_trade_ratio/net_depth_removal_excess_trade_ratio.cpp`, `src/sdk/math/ema.hpp`.

The complete train range check also finds AsymmetricSweepImpact up to approximately 1.623e32, with 10,437 projected finite origins above 1e9. Its source divides separate buy-side and sell-side impact EMAs. Each EMA advances only on its own classified sweep events, including zero price delta; the denominator can decay under repeated zero-impact sell events. Thus the numerator and denominator are estimated on different event clocks. A large ratio alone is not verified buy/sell impact asymmetry or a float32 overflow; its current source and values are retained in the frozen comparison.

SignedPricePathTortuosity has 129 projected finite train origins above absolute 1e9 and a native maximum near 6.333e13. Its implementation already uses retained endpoints rather than repeated add/remove of returns, but those endpoint mids still originate from `(bid + ask) / 2` in floating point. A read-only native-layout diagnostic on 2481, 2026-01-19, finds 124 physically closed 32-event paths with nonzero floating endpoint differences before the checked origin. For example, equal four-decimal quote midpoint keys can produce 91.950000000000003 versus 91.949999999999989, a denominator of approximately 1.421e-14. At origin 1768787040000000, both the unchanged native Parquet and the diagnostic's original-formula reproduction give exactly 14073748835533.0. This is direct evidence of quote-mid rounding amplified by division, not evidence of an extreme directional market path. Current source fixes do not retroactively repair frozen feature artifacts. Any correction requires a versioned native source, affected-column verification and fresh model-input lineage.

The float32 extremum check finds no supported column whose distinct native minimum and maximum collapse to the same float32 value. This does not prove preservation of every distinct intermediate value. Except for the separately excluded overflow column, extreme finite values are not clipped or replaced in model inputs.

Sources: `src/oms/modules/feature/microstructure/execution/asymmetric_sweep_impact/asymmetric_sweep_impact.cpp`, `src/oms/modules/feature/price_action/momentum/signed_price_path_tortuosity/signed_price_path_tortuosity.cpp`, `src/oms/model/quote.cpp`. The read-only reproduction is in `mid-rounding-audit-1/receipt.json`, `closed-decimal-paths.csv` and `audit.log`; it creates no features or labels.

## Relative features can exist without reaching the model

The first completed 16-symbol native day contains no finite DailyAnchor value. DailyContext's five signal columns are all NaN and its coverage is identically zero. This is a finding about this material recipe, not proof that those producers fail under every configuration.

`DailyAnchor` reads prior `DailyStats` from ContractApi. `DailyContext` reads prior stats or prior close metadata and requires ten valid adjacent returns. The market-panel importer in this experiment loads only the current day's generic contract BasicInfo. BasicInfo provides tick/limits and trading metadata but does not populate prior close or historical daily stats. The ordinary canonical stock importer also sets `HistoryDays: 0`, so merely adding a DailyContext declaration does not supply its required history. Missing calendar slots are preserved by the consumers.

The frozen experiment therefore measures the available intraday pool. A separate future hypothesis is that causal, dated, native daily/session baselines restore useful cross-day relative representation. It needs a native producer, full side-input/cache lineage, the 2026 date floor and an availability/ablation comparison. No history before 2026, missing-day replacement or external download is authorized.

Sources: `AstraResearch/astra/market.py`, `src/oms/modules/contract_importer/contract_importer.cpp`, `src/oms/modules/feature/context/daily_anchor/daily_anchor.cpp`, `src/oms/modules/feature/context/daily_context/daily_context.cpp`, `src/oms/modules/tw/info_history.hpp`, `src/oms/api/contract_api.cpp`.

## Current hypotheses and competing explanations

FlowResponseSurprise uses strictly prior completed intervals to fit joint signed-flow/price response, with flow scaled by origin touch depth and elapsed seconds. Its current response residual is evaluated before adding that interval to the reference. Existing price/volatility context, normalized-flow-only, response-only and flow/reference controls separate possible sources of incremental information. This is an observational representation; it does not identify an intervention or latent order intent.

On the engineering day only 53.4 percent of origins have finite new flow/response values. A five-second quote gap resets its reference, so slow symbols may be poorly supported. All comparison arms keep those same origins and native missing states. Any apparent gain must be checked for symbol/activity concentration and availability effects before a mechanism claim.

CrossReturnContext is an existing producer. Its peer coverage denotes an observed continuous held mid, rather than a quote updated within a freshness bound. An unchanged or old observation remains in the calculation. The cross-symbol hypothesis therefore competes with nonsynchronous observation and target momentum; target-only context and within-symbol-day comparisons are required. A positive fixed-family ranking result alone does not verify directional catch-up as a cause.

Existing KyleLambda already fits a flow/price slope and R-squared after trade/book response episodes. Existing QueueConditionedFlowSurprise estimates a marginal flow innovation within depth/age-alignment cells, but requires external source bindings and is absent from the canonical template pool. PressureResponseState already observes attack, recovery and response episodes. The newly proposed representation's narrower difference is a realized response innovation under a strictly prior joint flow/price baseline; naming it differently is not independent evidence of novelty or gain.

The source parser sets TWSE and TAIFEX `Trade.side` by quote-rule classification against a prior continuous book. It is not a directly observed initiating trader's identity. CausalTradeSideInference adds quote/tick-rule heuristics and conflict/ambiguity confidence. The new module's known-share condition therefore means share supported by this observation proxy, not verified economic aggressor volume. Native snapshots and prints cannot identify intent, hidden liquidity, cancellations, or an intervention.

Sources: `src/oms/modules/feature/microstructure/liquidity/estimators/kyle_lambda/kyle_lambda.cpp`, `src/oms/modules/feature/microstructure/liquidity/queue_conditioned_flow_surprise/queue_conditioned_flow_surprise.cpp`, `src/oms/modules/feature/microstructure/liquidity/pressure_response_state/pressure_response_state.cpp`, `src/marketdata/parsers/twse_parser.cpp`, `src/marketdata/parsers/taifex_parser.cpp`, `src/oms/modules/feature/order_flow/trade_flow/trade_side_inference.h`.

## Exact tick-boundary arithmetic

EXF uses native tick size 0.05. Its native mid-tick coordinate is calculated with inverse tick size and an offset; floating-point contraction can affect subtraction at an exactly physical five-tick boundary. A native 2026-02-25 diagnostic found one 60-second endpoint at 4.999999999992724 among 27 values very near +5. The strict native indicator and native continuous endpoint agree on excluding it under their current arithmetic contract. No Python label replacement or silent native threshold tolerance was introduced. A separately versioned native boundary correction and fresh labels would be required to establish a robust physical-tick contract.

A complete train-label boundary scan uses a 1e-8 diagnostic band only to count candidate arithmetic edges; it does not change native labels. Stock and TXF train labels have no nonexact values within that band around either five-tick boundary at any of the four horizons. EXF at 300 seconds has 48 just inside, 51 just outside and 1,050 exactly on the two boundaries. This is bounded evidence about the observed training material, not proof about all future prices or tick-ladder bands.

The user-defined five-tick endpoint has very different support across markets. At 300 seconds, stock train has 120,231 events among 2,253,792 origins (5.3 percent), TXF has 128,447 among 143,574 known endpoints (89.5 percent), and EXF has 132,177 among 143,574 known endpoints (92.1 percent). The unchanged futures target is predominantly a directional endpoint task. A gain there is not an independent replication of rare large-move detection in stocks. The thresholds remain exactly the user's five ticks, and panels are reported separately.
