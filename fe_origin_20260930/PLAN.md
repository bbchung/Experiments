# FE origin research, 2026-09-30

The user authorized source-level re-examination and empirical strengthening of native C++ features, with preliminary results within 24 hours. This is pure-signal development research. No execution, fee, or profitability criterion decides feature acceptance.

All source observations, calibration and history inputs start on or after 2026-01-01. Missing symbol-days and contract-days are omitted in their original date slots. No older replacement days, repaired source data, or shifted role boundaries are allowed.

The native endpoint is `tick(mid(t+h)) - tick(mid(t))`, using the contract's piecewise tick ladder. Horizons are 60, 120, 180 and 300 seconds. Labels are up for endpoint >= 5 ticks, down for endpoint <= -5 ticks, otherwise none. A temporary excursion that reverses before the endpoint is not an event. Unavailable or unmatured endpoints remain missing.

The stock panel is fixed before fitting. It covers large technology names, mid-cap technology, shipping and display/memory stocks. Selection does not use forward outcomes, spreads, fees or day-trading eligibility. This is a bounded first panel, not a claim to represent every stock. TAIFEX is evaluated separately, retaining native monthly contract identity; TXF/MXF are not counted as independent economic replications.

The canonical `astra.workflows.material` produces the train/tune/forward artifacts. Its sampler is periodic, every ten seconds. The calendar is cropped at 2026-01-01 so native daily-history consumers cannot read older trading days. The final one-day baseline slot is unused; no result is described as pristine or final OOS. Earlier research exposure does not disappear when run directories are pruned.

Controls: (1) native price, volatility, time and tick-scale inputs; (2) every usable exported native feature; (3) the existing train-only PMQ; (4) a train-only tail-oriented selector. Compare all arms on identical row keys and finite native labels. Feature availability is retained as native states, not repaired with fabricated values.

The exact existing QualityOnlySelectionKernel also selects a separately identified arm from the same PMQ study, retaining its quality/support guards while omitting IC and redundancy rejection. It is not silently equated with the broader all-usable current-feature control. Both PMQ and quality-only selections use three-class, native binary and signed-return RMSE objectives under identical training weights; fixed objective decisions apply to each selector. Selector interpretations compare these recorded universes with the full native-extension arm, with no claim that a selector gain alone identifies a new market mechanism.

Primary research question: does a representation improve both large-move ranking and direction beyond price/volatility context? Report two-sided AP, within-symbol-day ranking, correct versus opposite tail hits, support, concentration and fixed non-overlapping origins. Report all four horizons. Designate 300 seconds as the primary comparison before scoring; other horizons are supporting measurements. Day-block uncertainty is descriptive development evidence, not a general causal identification or multiple-search guarantee.

Candidate hypotheses, subject to a full equivalence check against the existing inventory:

- H1: persistent signed demand relative to displayed-liquidity recovery predicts endpoint displacement better than instantaneous imbalance. Ablate normalization and recovery separately; compare to existing pressure, refill and barrier-work features.
- H2: the deviation of realized price response from a strictly prior, comparable local response baseline separates absorption from loss of resilience. Control for recent returns, volatility, depth and activity. Every response episode must have completed before the feature's origin.
- H3: common market innovations not yet reflected in the target predict directional catch-up. Compare to existing CrossReturnContext, target momentum and volatility-only cross context. Use time-displaced and direction-neutral controls.
- H4: single-column IC screening discards conditional/tail information. Compare quality-only inclusion, existing PMQ and a predeclared tail-oriented selector. All selection and fitting use train; tune/calibration nominates a bounded challenger before forward scoring.

The mechanism motivation is conditional, not a claim that past papers identify the effect in Taiwan. [Cont, Kukanov and Stoikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402) motivate combining signed demand with displayed depth rather than relying on trade size alone. [Bouchaud, Farmer and Lillo](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.0822) motivate testing the joint dynamics of persistent demand and changing liquidity. H2 tests whether an unexpected response, given strictly prior local flow/price coupling, carries incremental endpoint information; its representation is P1 and does not identify an intervention or latent cancellation.

[Lo and MacKinlay](https://www.mit.edu/~alo/Papers/lo-mackinlay-90b.html) motivate H3's lead/lag alternative, while [their nonsynchronous trading analysis](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/205304fa-fca3-4cef-b0d7-b4486549dfdc) motivates the competing explanation: stale observation. Native row clocks, peer coverage, target-price controls and within-symbol-day comparisons therefore accompany every cross-symbol result.
